# Oscillatory Sign-Switching Dark Energy - Master's Thesis
### Author's Corrected Version (2026)

This document is a corrected version of the Master's dissertation originally submitted in 2026 to the University of Coimbra, Portugal.

The corrections consist primarily of grammatical improvements, together with the correction of the order of two figures and their corresponding descriptions. These changes do not affect the numerical results, methodology, or scientific conclusions of the original dissertation.

The research was conducted at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), Spain, through an Erasmus+ traineeship.

The officially submitted version remains available through the University of Coimbra's institutional repository.
